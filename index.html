FAQ

Straight answers.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Local and service businesses across Philly, New Jersey, and New York: salons, barbershops, restaurants, lounges, nail spas, and more. If you sell to customers in your area, we can help.

Do I have to come to you for shoots?

No. We come to you. Shoots happen at your place of business or around your neighborhood, scheduled at your convenience.

What happens on the free strategy call?

We look at how you're getting customers now, find the biggest gap, and show you a plan to fix it. If it makes sense to work together, we'll walk you through the right package. No pressure.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. Any package can be split into three monthly payments.

Do you do marketing, production, and consulting separately?

Yes. Every package is available for any one of the three, or we can combine them. Most clients get the best results when all three work together.
